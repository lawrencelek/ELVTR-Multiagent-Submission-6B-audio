# Prototype 10.3: the side sensors

- **Build:** `prototypes/p10/dummies_p10_3.html`, level `junction`, stage 10.3. Headless: `node qa/testroad.js --stage 10.3 --out qa/baseline/p10_3.json`.
- **Runs:** 15 bots × 2 mode(s) × offsets [0, 7, 13] s × 300 sim-seconds; escalation 600 s.
- **Mode `lesson`:** from the build's start (10.2: rung 0, on foot; 10.3: rung 1, Battery 1 fitted); the lesson and the part as the build delivers them.
- **Mode `trained`:** at the top rung from the start, Battery 1 fitted: the rung's own table.
- **Cover:** aim, flank, jump and dashjump wait at (300, 410), behind the barrier: cover.
- **The build's values:** `duel.enabled` 1, `duel.carsOnRoad` 1, `duel.redReturnSeconds` 4, `proto.learningEnabled` 0, `proto.learn.standoffEnabled` 0, `perception.forwardLength` 720, `proto.driver.cautionZone` 180, `run.appearances.road` 1000, `run.quota.road` 1000000, `duel.topRung` 2, `duel.standstillSpeed` 5, `duel.dashArrives` 3, `duel.startRung` 1, `duel.jumpArrives` 2, `perception.sideNoticeSeconds` 0.6.
