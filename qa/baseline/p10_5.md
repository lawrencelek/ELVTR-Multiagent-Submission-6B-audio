# Prototype 10.5: the street cameras

- **Build:** `prototypes/p10/dummies_p10_5.html`, level `junction`, stage 10.5. Headless: `node qa/testroad.js --stage 10.5 --out qa/baseline/p10_5.json`.
- **Runs:** 17 bots × 2 mode(s) × offsets [0, 7, 13] s × 300 sim-seconds; escalation 600 s.
- **Mode `lesson`:** from the build's start (10.2: rung 0, on foot; 10.3: rung 1, Battery 1 fitted; 10.5: rung 3, Battery 1 and Legs 1 fitted); the lesson and the part as the build delivers them.
- **Mode `trained`:** at the top rung from the start, every part its rungs bring fitted: the rung's own table.
- **Cover:** aim, flank, jump and dashjump wait at (300, 410), behind the barrier: cover; conebait starts at the gap (188, 414); lurk waits at (660, 576), past every field's edge.
- **Parts:** Battery 1 and Legs 1 fitted from the start; Hands 1, the cones, by drone at the first stop for a dummy behind cover.
- **The build's values:** `duel.enabled` 1, `duel.carsOnRoad` 1, `duel.redReturnSeconds` 4, `proto.learningEnabled` 0, `proto.learn.standoffEnabled` 0, `perception.forwardLength` 720, `proto.driver.cautionZone` 180, `run.appearances.road` 1000, `run.quota.road` 1000000, `duel.topRung` 4, `duel.standstillSpeed` 5, `duel.dashArrives` 3, `duel.startRung` 3, `duel.jumpArrives` 3, `perception.sideNoticeSeconds` 0.6, `duel.conesArrives` 2.
