# Prototype 10.8: the search

- **Build:** `prototypes/p10/dummies_p10_8.html`, level `junction`, stage 10.8. Headless: `node qa/testroad.js --stage 10.8 --out qa/baseline/p10_8.json`.
- **Runs:** 23 bots × 2 mode(s) × offsets [0, 7, 13] s × 300 sim-seconds; escalation 600 s.
- **Mode `lesson`:** from the build's start (10.2: rung 0, on foot; 10.3: rung 1, Battery 1 fitted; 10.5: rung 3, Battery 1 and Legs 1 fitted); the lesson and the part as the build delivers them.
- **Mode `trained`:** at the top rung from the start, every part its rungs bring fitted: the rung's own table.
- **Cover:** aim, flank, jump and dashjump wait at (300, 410), behind the barrier: cover; conebait starts at the gap (188, 414); planter and standplanter use one spot behind the planter row south of road A, east (590, 418); roam, peek and peekmove use nine spots behind the three rows; lurk waits at (660, 576).
