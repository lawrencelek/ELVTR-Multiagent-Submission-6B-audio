# Prototype 10.8: the search

- **Build:** `prototypes/p10/dummies_p10_8.html`, level `junction`, stage 10.8. Headless: `node qa/testroad.js --stage 10.8 --out qa/baseline/p10_8.json`.
- **Runs:** 22 bots × 2 mode(s) × offsets [0, 7, 13] s × 300 sim-seconds; escalation 600 s.
- **Mode `lesson`:** from the build's start (10.2: rung 0, on foot; 10.3: rung 1, Battery 1 fitted; 10.5: rung 3, Battery 1 and Legs 1 fitted); the lesson and the part as the build delivers them.
- **Mode `trained`:** at the top rung from the start, every part its rungs bring fitted: the rung's own table.
- **Cover:** aim, flank, jump and dashjump wait at (300, 410), behind the barrier: cover; conebait starts at the gap (188, 414); planter and standplanter use one spot behind the planter row south of road A, east (590, 418); roam, peek and peekmove use nine spots behind the three rows; lurk waits at (660, 576).
- **Parts:** Battery 1, Legs 1, Hands 1 and DUCK fitted from the start. No new part. The car starts at rung 6, crash memory; from rung 7 it searches where it last saw the dummy.
- **The build's values:** `duel.enabled` 1, `duel.carsOnRoad` 1, `duel.redReturnSeconds` 2, `proto.learningEnabled` 0, `proto.learn.standoffEnabled` 0, `perception.forwardLength` 720, `proto.driver.cautionZone` 180, `run.appearances.road` 1000, `run.quota.road` 1000000, `duel.topRung` 7, `duel.standstillSpeed` 5, `duel.dashArrives` 3, `duel.startRung` 6, `duel.jumpArrives` 3, `perception.sideNoticeSeconds` 0.6, `duel.conesArrives` 3, `duel.duckArrives` 3, `duel.planters` 1, `props.planter.height` 14, `body.duckHeight` 6, `movement.duckSpeedFactor` 0.35, `perception.duckNoticeFactor` 1.5, `perception.sensorHeight` 14, `perception.lidarHeight` 26, `perception.streetCameraHeight` 60, `perception.streetCameraRange` 180, `duel.memoryRadius` 50, `duel.memorySites` 3, `duel.runInLength` 200, `proto.health.standard` 500, `duel.blockSeconds` 10, `duel.searchRadius` 60, `duel.searchSeconds` 4.
