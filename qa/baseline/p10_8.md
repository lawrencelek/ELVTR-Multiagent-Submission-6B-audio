- **Build:** `prototypes/p10/dummies_p10_8.html`, level `junction`, stage 10.8. Headless: `node qa/testroad.js --stage 10.8 --out qa/baseline/p10_8.json`.
- **Runs:** 23 bots × 2 mode(s) × offsets [0, 7, 13] s × 300 sim-seconds; escalation 600 s.
- **Mode `lesson`:** from the build's start (10.2: rung 0, on foot; 10.3: rung 1, Battery 1 fitted; 10.5: rung 3, Battery 1 and Legs 1 fitted); the lesson and the part as the build delivers them.
