Prototype 10 playtest: one page per test

Each test changes one thing about the red car. Play them in order. Every page except the newest is frozen, so it stays as it was tested. Session logs: press L in any test.

10.0 · the baseline: Prototype 9 as builtNo new build: 10.0 measured this. Sasha's first session. Look for: does standing in the road still get you hit? 10.1 · the test road (frozen)One Monument junction, one red car that senses and learns nothing. On foot. Look for: do you play on for 3 minutes, and can you name a trick? 10.2 · the first lesson (frozen)Your first hit on its nose becomes its training clip; it returns with a forward camera and brakes for what is ahead. The dash arrives by drone. Look for: can you say why it brakes, and do you find the flank? 10.3 · the side sensors (frozen)It starts with the camera; you start with the dash. A hit from the side teaches it to watch its flanks: it stops for you anywhere beside the road. The jump arrives by drone. Look for: do you find cover, or the plinth, without being told? 10.4 · the roof lidar (frozen)It starts with its side sensors; you start with the dash and the jump. A hit from above in plain sight (a jump, or a dash off the plinth; not out of cover) teaches it to see height: the plinth no longer hides you. Look for: do you notice it now stops for you up there, and turn to cover? 10.5 · the street cameras (frozen)It starts with its lidar; you start with the dash and the jump. A hit out of cover teaches it to link to the junction's cameras: it stops for you behind the barrier too. The cones arrive by drone; E picks one up and puts it down. Look for: do you lay a cone in its lane, and strike while it swerves round it? 10.6 · DUCK (frozen)It starts with the street cameras; you start with the dash, the jump and the cones. A hit while it swerves round a cone teaches it to stop at cones instead. DUCK arrives by drone: hold C. Ducked behind a planter row you are hidden; a dash vaults the row. Look for: do you read HIDDEN, and spring over the row as it passes? 10.7 · crash memory (frozen)It starts reading road items; you start with all four parts and a lighter body (500). Every crash leaves a chalk outline on the road. Strike twice from the same spot, from cover, and it learns your habit: it rings the last three spots and checks them as it drives. Keep it waiting and a drone lifts you aside. Look for: do you read the red rings, and move to a new spot? ▶ 10.8 · the searchIt starts with crash memory. Let it see you, duck out of sight and strike within 3 seconds, and it learns to search: it leaves an amber ghost where it last saw you, stops short of it, and finds you anywhere inside its ring. After 4 seconds it gives up and drives on. The street cameras now see a 60° cone each, panning back and forth. Each strike is graded UNSEEN, LOST or SEEN. Lure the car: it stops where it expects you, and draws its stop line. Look for: do you read the ghost, and crawl out of the ring before you strike?
